Load the `grill` skill and apply it to: $ARGUMENTS

Do not start building. Interrogate first.

- Read whatever already exists — the codebase, existing files, design tokens, conventions — before asking anything you could have found out.
- Ask three or four questions per round, with concrete options and the trade-off attached. Mark your recommendation.
- Report your confidence as a percentage after each round.
- Stop the moment I say "you decide", or show impatience. Then list every assumption you are making and build the interesting version, not the safe one.
- When you clear ~90%, write the six-line brief and wait for one confirmation before starting.
